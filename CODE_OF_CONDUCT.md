# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, constructive and considerate in issues, reviews, documentation and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical choices with evidence and acknowledge different perspectives.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not personal judgments about its author.
- Respect privacy, consent and maintainers' time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **tobias.waelde@gmail.com**. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or exclude participants when necessary. They will consider context, protect reporter privacy where possible and apply proportionate action. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the organization publicly.
